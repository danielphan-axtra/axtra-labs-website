Client background

A multi-country professional-services firm with 4,000+ employees managed HR policy across SharePoint libraries, email threads and an aging ATS. Recruiters spent hours hunting for answers, and people-ops could not trust a single source of truth when policy changed.

Key challenges

  • Policy PDFs, FAQs and ATS notes lived in separate systems with inconsistent permissions.
  • Recruiters asked the same questions repeatedly; answers depended on who was online.
  • No citations — employees could not verify whether an answer matched the current handbook.
  • Talent search was keyword-only, so skill matches and past project experience were easy to miss.

Product screenshots

What we built

  • RAG chatbot over SharePoint HR docs and curated talent profiles, with role-based access.
  • Citation panel that links every answer back to the source paragraph.
  • Talent search that ranks candidates by skills, projects and availability signals.
  • Admin console for ingesting new policies and retiring outdated versions.

Project team: 6 engineers across AI/ML, backend and domain specialists — delivery over 14 weeks.

How we delivered
