AI-Powered Retail Traffic Analytics

Footfall and dwell-time analytics from existing CCTV for a regional mall operator in Thailand.

AI-Powered RFP & Tender Response PlatformIntelligent Loan Processing Platform

Key Details

ChallengeA regional shopping-mall operator needed footfall and dwell-time analytics from the CCTV it already had, rather than a new sensor install.
SolutionMulti-stream analytics on existing CCTV: people detection and tracking, a traffic engine for footfall and dwell time, and a heatmap dashboard.
TechnologiesYOLO, OpenCV, PyTorch, TensorFlow, Python, Node.js, React, TypeScript, FFmpeg, AWS EC2, S3, Lambda, Kafka, Redis

Technologies used

Client

The client is a regional shopping-mall operator in Thailand, running large retail and entertainment complexes across Southeast Asia. The work turns existing CCTV into footfall, dwell-time and queue analytics for layout and operations.

Product screenshots

What we built

  • Multi-stream analytics on existing CCTV, with no extra sensors
  • People detection, tracking and movement analysis
  • Traffic engine for customer flow, zone occupancy and dwell time in stores and corridors
  • Queue detection at checkouts and food courts
  • Heatmaps, traffic statistics, real-time alerts and historical analytics
