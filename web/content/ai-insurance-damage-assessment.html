Client

The client is a fast-growing InsurTech SaaS company in the United States. It provides digital claim processing for insurance providers and automotive partners, and wanted AI assessment of vehicle photos in the claim workflow.

What we built

  • Models that detect scratches, dents, broken parts and impact areas from uploaded photos, and classify severity
  • Claim intake, image validation and damage-assessment summaries
  • Workflow integration with claim management systems
  • Fraud checks for duplicate or suspicious images and abnormal claim patterns
  • Dashboards for claim tracking and AI analysis
