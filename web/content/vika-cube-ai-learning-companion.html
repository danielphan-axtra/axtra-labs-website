Vika Cube AI Learning Companion

Embedded voice companion and a 2.4-inch interface for a children's learning device. Team of 3 over 6 months.

Key Details

Client

The client is a Hong Kong-based technology company. Vika Cube is a compact learning companion for children, combining voice, a 2.4-inch display and flows for learning, storytelling, games and exploration. The client company is not named.

Product screenshots

What we built

  • Embedded software for the display, buttons, speaker, microphone, LEDs and core device functions
  • Voice interaction so a child can speak to the companion and hear a reply
  • 2.4-inch TFT interface with animations during interaction
  • Hardware integration, including microphone, speaker, buttons, status LEDs, display, USB-C charging and device controls
  • Companion flows for learning, storytelling, games, exploration and emotional companionship
  • Software tuned for a compact, low-power device
