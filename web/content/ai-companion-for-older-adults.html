AI Companion for Older Adults

Conversational companion device for older adults, with reminders, a touch UI and cloud connectivity.

Vika Cube AI Learning CompanionHome Robotics Engineering Platform

Key Details

ChallengeA Canada-based technology company needed device software for an older-adult companion: conversation, reminders and a touch UI, connected to the cloud.
SolutionEmbedded software with conversational AI and voice, reminder and wellness flows, a touch UI and cloud integration.
TechnologiesC/C++, Embedded Linux, Qt/QML, LLM, OpenAI GPT, Whisper, Text-to-Speech, Python, REST, WebSocket, MQTT, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, AWS IoT, S3, RDS, CloudWatch

Client

The client is a Canada-based technology company. The device is an in-home companion for older adults, with conversation, reminders, wellness support and a way to stay in touch with family and caregivers. The client company is not named.

What we built

  • Device software for control, system services, UI and interaction
  • Conversational AI and voice, including speech-to-text and text-to-speech
  • Flows for conversation, reminders, wellness, entertainment and personal engagement
  • Reminders for medication, hydration, activities and daily routines
  • Wellness activities such as exercise, mindfulness and check-ins
  • Messages, photos and video calls with family and caregivers
  • Touchscreen UI and character animations
  • Cloud connection for content, user data, device management and personalization
