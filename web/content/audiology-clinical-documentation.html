Key Details

ChallengeClinicians were producing notes from a spreadsheet system that held the forms, phrase fragments, insurance plans and procedure codes for audiology visits.
SolutionA web application that assembles clinical notes from that library, with e-signature and quotes. Practice-management integration was out of scope.
