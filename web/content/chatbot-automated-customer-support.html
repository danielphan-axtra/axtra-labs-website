Client background

A travel and hospitality brand flooded agents with the same FAQs while high-intent booking chats died in transcripts. Guests bounced between bot and human without context, and sales never saw which conversations were ready to convert.

Key challenges

  • FAQ volume drowned agents who should have focused on complex bookings.
  • Booking intent was buried in free-text chat with no structured handoff.
  • Legacy bot answers conflicted with current offers and policies.
  • No analytics on deflection vs. revenue-generating conversations.

Product screenshots

What we built

  • Production chatbot with retrieval over FAQs, offers and booking policies.
  • Tooling to start booking flows and pass structured state into the CRM.
  • Human handoff that preserves full transcript and guest intent.
  • Ops dashboard for deflection, containment and booking conversion.

Project team: 6 engineers across AI/ML, backend and domain specialists — delivery over 12 weeks.
