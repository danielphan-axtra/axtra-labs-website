Ads and Ambassador Platform for a UK E-commerce Brand

Ongoing build of an ads-to-revenue data foundation and an owned ambassador platform for a UK e-commerce brand.

AI-Powered Retail Traffic AnalyticsAI-Powered Skin Analysis Platform

Key Details

ChallengeAd, store and analytics data were reviewed separately, so ad spend could not be tied to revenue. The ambassador programme ran on a third-party app that the brand wanted to replace with a platform it owns.
SolutionTwo products in co-development: a data foundation that joins Meta ad spend to Shopify revenue, and an ambassador platform for recruiting, gifts, tasks, points and commission. Google Ads is a later phase.
TechnologiesClaude Code, GitHub, Slack, Shopify, Meta Ads, Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, PostgreSQL

Technologies used

Claude CodeGitHubSlackShopifyMeta AdsGoogle AnalyticsMicrosoft ClarityPostgreSQL

Client

The client is a UK direct-to-consumer e-commerce brand on Shopify. The engagement is ongoing. The client keeps Lovable for its internal operating system. The architecture is described as a modern web framework with a PostgreSQL database, background jobs and AI models accessed through Axtra's own layer. A specific application framework is not named.
