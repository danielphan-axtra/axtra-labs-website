Key Details

ChallengeA Singapore skincare startup needed skin analysis, product recommendations and a conversational copilot in one product.
SolutionA computer-vision engine for 20+ skin parameters, an AI recommendation engine, an LLM beauty copilot, a progress dashboard and REST APIs.
TechnologiesVision Transformer, YOLOv8, OpenCV, PyTorch, ONNX Runtime, Azure OpenAI GPT-4o, LangChain, RAG, FastAPI, NestJS, React, React Native, AWS, Docker, Kubernetes

Client

The client is a startup skincare and beauty brand in Singapore. Shoppers photograph their skin on a phone and receive an analysis, a routine and a beauty copilot.

What we built

  • Computer-vision engine for 20+ skin parameters, including acne, wrinkles, pores, pigmentation, redness, dark circles, texture, oiliness, hydration indicators and skin tone
  • Recommendation engine for skincare routines and products from the detected conditions
  • LLM beauty copilot that explains results and answers skincare questions
  • Customer dashboard that tracks skin progress from historical analysis
  • REST APIs for mobile apps and e-commerce
  • Analysis from a selfie taken on a smartphone camera

Engagement

Delivery was time and materials with 7 engineers over 12 months.
