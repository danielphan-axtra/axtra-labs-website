Digital Identity and KYC Platform

KYC and digital identity for a German RegTech scale-up. MVP in under 4 months, and onboarding in under 2 minutes.

Intelligent Loan Processing PlatformAI Insurance Damage Assessment

Key Details

ChallengeA German RegTech company offering KYC as a service to fintech and insurtech firms needed a fast, compliant digital-identity product that partners can embed.
SolutionA microservices platform with face liveness and matching, document verification, risk scoring, an admin console, and a JavaScript SDK with a sandbox.
TechnologiesNestJS, Vue.js, PostgreSQL, Azure Face API, Azure App Services, Azure Blob, Azure AD, Docker, Azure Monitor, Application Insights, Azure DevOps

Client

The client is a German RegTech scale-up. It offers KYC as a service for fintech and insurtech companies. The client is not named here.

Product screenshots

What we built

  • NestJS backend and Vue.js admin on a microservices architecture
  • Face liveness and face matching
  • Document-verification APIs and a risk-scoring engine
  • Containers on Azure App Services, with Azure AD and role-based access
  • CI/CD with audit logging
  • JavaScript SDK, Swagger and Postman docs, and a sandbox

Engagement

Delivery was an offshore development center engagement with 5 people: one project manager, two backend engineers, one frontend engineer and one tester, over 12 months.

Project team: 5 engineers. Duration: 12 months. Model: OSDC.

Outcome

The MVP was delivered in under 4 months. KYC onboarding takes under 2 minutes. The platform is described as compliant with GDPR, eIDAS and ISO 27001. Partners integrated through the SDK within 2 weeks.
