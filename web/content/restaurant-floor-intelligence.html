Key Details

ChallengeA US startup building an AI restaurant-operations platform needed a live view of the floor from CCTV, tied to POS and reservations.
SolutionComputer vision on CCTV for real-time events, task routing, an operations assistant, POS and reservation data, and dashboards.

Client

The client is a US-based technology startup building an AI platform for restaurant operations. It runs on cameras the restaurant already has, and combines that video with POS, reservation and staffing data. The reference describes it as an AI floor manager for full-service restaurants.

Product screenshots

What we built

  • Video analysis of existing CCTV to read floor activity
  • Event detection for tables ready to seat, guests waiting, tables ready to bus, and checks waiting to close
  • Task routing to hosts, servers and bussers
  • An operations assistant using staffing, sales and service data
  • CCTV insights joined with POS, reservation and staffing data
  • Live dashboards for floor state, alerts and management analytics
