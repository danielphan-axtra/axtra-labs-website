AI-Powered Internal HR Knowledge & Talent Search Chatbot

An internal chatbot that searches HR policy and talent profiles so recruiters find answers in seconds.

AI-Powered RFP & Tender Response PlatformChatbot for Automated Customer SupportOptimizing GPT-4 Prompt Engineering

Key Details

ChallengeHR teams could not find policy answers or candidate skills quickly across fragmented drives and ATS notes.
SolutionAxtra Labs built a RAG chatbot over HR documents and talent profiles, with access control and citations.
TechnologiesAzure OpenAI, LangChain, SharePoint, Elasticsearch

Technologies used

Azure OpenAI LangChain SharePoint Elasticsearch Python

Client background

A multi-country professional-services firm with 4,000+ employees managed HR policy across SharePoint libraries, email threads and an aging ATS. Recruiters spent hours hunting for answers, and people-ops could not trust a single source of truth when policy changed.

Key challenges

  • Policy PDFs, FAQs and ATS notes lived in separate systems with inconsistent permissions.
  • Recruiters asked the same questions repeatedly; answers depended on who was online.
  • No citations — employees could not verify whether an answer matched the current handbook.
  • Talent search was keyword-only, so skill matches and past project experience were easy to miss.

What we built

  • RAG chatbot over SharePoint HR docs and curated talent profiles, with role-based access.
  • Citation panel that links every answer back to the source paragraph.
  • Talent search that ranks candidates by skills, projects and availability signals.
  • Admin console for ingesting new policies and retiring outdated versions.

Project team: 6 engineers across AI/ML, backend and domain specialists — delivery over 14 weeks.

How we delivered

01

Discover

Mapped HR workflows, permission boundaries and the highest-volume question types.

02

Ingest

Normalized SharePoint and ATS exports into a searchable index with ACL metadata.

03

Pilot

Rolled out to HR ops first, then recruiters, with feedback loops on answer quality.

04

Harden

Added eval sets, access audits and a change process for policy updates.
